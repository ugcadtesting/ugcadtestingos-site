Order records are retained only as long as needed for delivery, support, accounting, dispute handling and legal compliance. Personal data is not sold. It is shared only with service providers or authorities when necessary for these purposes.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, or object to certain processing. Contact us using the email above. You may also complain to your competent data protection authority.

Analytics and cookies

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits, traffic sources and clicks that begin the Stripe checkout. Analytics storage is denied by default and is enabled only after you select “Allow analytics”. You can keep essential-only use or reopen Cookie settings from the site footer at any time. Advertising storage, ad personalisation and advertising-user-data signals remain disabled.

Essential hosting and checkout technologies may still be used to provide the pages and secure payment flow. Google may process limited technical and usage information according to its own privacy terms when analytics consent is granted.
