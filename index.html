FAQ

Questions Before You Start

What you need to know about the system and how it fits into your workflow.

Is it just a spreadsheet?+

No. The editable Google Sheets dashboard is one of seven tools. The kit also includes a fatigue decision tree, a testing playbook, a performance brief, hook starters, proof patterns and AI prompts. You import an ad-level CSV export from Meta Ads Manager (or type your numbers in); it is not a live ad-platform connection.

Do I need a large ad budget?+

No. The framework helps you make clearer decisions with the budget you have. It does not promise universal statistical significance.

Can I use it with Meta and TikTok Ads?+

Yes. The dashboard includes a ready-made import for Meta Ads Manager CSV exports. For TikTok or other channels, paste your numbers under the same column headers. The decision framework is built around creative variables and your brand's thresholds.

What if I already use Notion or Airtable?+

Keep using them. This system defines what to capture, what to compare and which decision to make next—not where every file must live.

Can't I just use ChatGPT?+

AI can draft options. The OS gives you the framework to decide what to test, against which control and how to turn the result into the next brief.

Is it for creators or agencies?+

It is primarily built for creative strategists, media buyers, agency founders and DTC growth teams. Performance-focused creators can also use it to make their work more testable.

How quickly can I start?+

You can start immediately with the included setup guide, then build your first hypothesis, matched variation and test queue.

Is the system customizable?+

Yes. It uses configurable fields and brand-level thresholds instead of forcing every team into one benchmark.

What exactly do I receive?+

Seven tools: Creative Testing Dashboard, Fatigue Decision Tree, Creative Testing Playbook, Performance UGC Brief, 100 Hook Starters, 30 Proof Patterns and 15 AI Creative Strategist Prompts. The Variable Map, Hook × Proof × Offer Matrix and Creative Evidence Loop are parts of the method, not extra standalone files.

How is the product delivered?+

After your payment is confirmed, the download link is sent automatically to the email address used at checkout. If it does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam or contact support.
